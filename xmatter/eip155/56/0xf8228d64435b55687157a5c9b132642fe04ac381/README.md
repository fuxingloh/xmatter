NuScale Power Corporation xStock (SMRx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. SMRx tracks the price of NuScale Power Corporation. SMRx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of NuScale Power Corporation, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
