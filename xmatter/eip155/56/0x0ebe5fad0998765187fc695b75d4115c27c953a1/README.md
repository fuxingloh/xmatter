KRAQ xStock (KRAQx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. KRAQx tracks the price of KRAKacquisition. KRAQx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of KRAKacquisition, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
