KLA Corporation xStock (KLACx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. KLACx tracks the price of KLA Corporation. KLACx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of KLA Corporation, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
