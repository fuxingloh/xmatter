VanEck Agribusiness ETF xStock (MOOx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. MOOx tracks the price of VanEck Agribusiness ETF. MOOx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of VanEck Agribusiness ETF, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
