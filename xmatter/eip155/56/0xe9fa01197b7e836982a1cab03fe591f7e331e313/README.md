Applied Materials, Inc. xStock (AMATx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. AMATx tracks the price of Applied Materials, Inc.. AMATx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Applied Materials, Inc., whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
