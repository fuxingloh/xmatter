Sandisk Corporation xStock (SNDKx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. SNDKx tracks the price of Sandisk Corporation. SNDKx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Sandisk Corporation, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
