Fundrise Innovation Fund, LLC xStock (VCXx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. VCXx tracks the price of Fundrise Innovation Fund, LLC. VCXx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Fundrise Innovation Fund, LLC, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
