iShares Silver Trust xStock (SLVx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. SLVx tracks the price of iShares Silver Trust. SLVx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of iShares Silver Trust, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
