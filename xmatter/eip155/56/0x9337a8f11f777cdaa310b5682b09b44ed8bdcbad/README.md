Vanguard FTSE Europe ETF xStock (VGKx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. VGKx tracks the price of Vanguard FTSE Europe ETF. VGKx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Vanguard FTSE Europe ETF, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
