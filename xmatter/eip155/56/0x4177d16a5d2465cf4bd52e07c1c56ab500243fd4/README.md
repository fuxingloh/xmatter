Lam Research Corporation xStock (LRCXx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. LRCXx tracks the price of Lam Research Corporation. LRCXx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Lam Research Corporation, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
