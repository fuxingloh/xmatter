VanEck Semiconductor ETF xStock (SMHx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. SMHx tracks the price of VanEck Semiconductor ETF. SMHx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of VanEck Semiconductor ETF, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
