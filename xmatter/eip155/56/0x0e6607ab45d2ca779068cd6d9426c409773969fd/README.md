Global X Uranium ETF xStock (URAx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. URAx tracks the price of Global X Uranium ETF. URAx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Global X Uranium ETF, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
