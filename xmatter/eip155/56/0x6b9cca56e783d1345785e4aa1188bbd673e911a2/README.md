SPDR S&P Oil & Gas Exploration & Production ETF xStock (XOPx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. XOPx tracks the price of State Street SPDR S&P Oil & Gas Exploration & Production ETF. XOPx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of State Street SPDR S&P Oil & Gas Exploration & Production ETF, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
