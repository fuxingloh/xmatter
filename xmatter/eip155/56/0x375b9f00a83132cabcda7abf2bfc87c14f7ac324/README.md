iShares U.S. Aerospace & Defense ETF xStock (ITAx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. ITAx tracks the price of iShares U.S. Aerospace & Defense ETF. ITAx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of iShares U.S. Aerospace & Defense ETF, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
