Eaton Corporation plc xStock (ETNx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. ETNx tracks the price of Eaton Corporation plc. ETNx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Eaton Corporation plc, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
