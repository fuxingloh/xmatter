abrdn Physical Platinum Shares xStock (PPLTx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. PPLTx tracks the price of abrdn Physical Platinum Shares ETF. PPLTx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of abrdn Physical Platinum Shares ETF, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
