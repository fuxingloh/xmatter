Constellation Energy Corporation xStock (CEGx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. CEGx tracks the price of Constellation Energy Corporation. CEGx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Constellation Energy Corporation, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
