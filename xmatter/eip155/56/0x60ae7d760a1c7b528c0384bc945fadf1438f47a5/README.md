Bitgo xStock (BTGOx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. BTGOx tracks the price of Bitgo Holdings Inc. BTGOx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Bitgo Holdings Inc., whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
