Energy Select Sector SPDR Fund xStock (XLEx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. XLEx tracks the price of State Street Energy Select Sector SPDR Fund. XLEx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of State Street Energy Select Sector SPDR Fund, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
