Super Micro Computer, Inc. xStock (SMCIx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. SMCIx tracks the price of Super Micro Computer, Inc.. SMCIx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Super Micro Computer, Inc., whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
