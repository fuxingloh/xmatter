Applied Digital Corporation xStock (APLDx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. APLDx tracks the price of Applied Digital Corporation. APLDx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Applied Digital Corporation, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
