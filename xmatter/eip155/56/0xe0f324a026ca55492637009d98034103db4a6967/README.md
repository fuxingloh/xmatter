Brera xStock (SLMTx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. SLMTx tracks the price of Brera Holdings PLC. SLMTx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Brera Holdings PLC, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
