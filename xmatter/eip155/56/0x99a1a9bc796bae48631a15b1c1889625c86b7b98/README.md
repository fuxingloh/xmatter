Teradyne, Inc. xStock (TERx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. TERx tracks the price of Teradyne, Inc.. TERx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Teradyne, Inc., whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
