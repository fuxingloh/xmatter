Dell Technologies Inc. xStock (DELLx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. DELLx tracks the price of Dell Technologies Inc.. DELLx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Dell Technologies Inc., whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
