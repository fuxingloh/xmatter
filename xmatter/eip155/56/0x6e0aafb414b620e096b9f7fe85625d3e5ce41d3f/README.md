Vanguard Growth ETF xStock (VUGx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. VUGx tracks the price of Vanguard Growth ETF. VUGx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of Vanguard Growth ETF, whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
