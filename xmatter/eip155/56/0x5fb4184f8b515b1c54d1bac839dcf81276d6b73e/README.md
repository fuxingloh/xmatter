GE Vernova Inc. xStock (GEVx) is a tracker certificate issued as a freely transferable token on selected blockchains. GEVx tracks the price of GE Vernova Inc.. GEVx is designed to give eligible investors regulatory-compliant access to the stock price of GE Vernova Inc., whilst maintaining the benefits of blockchain technology.
